How to play GuessRack

  1. One player hits Create a room and reads the four-character code to the other.
  2. The second player types that code and joins. Each of you plays on your own device.
  3. You both see the same rack of 24 word cards, and each of you is secretly given one word from it, shown on the card in the corner of your screen.
  4. On your turn, ask one yes/no question out loud — Is it alive? Is it man-made? Would it fit in a backpack? Your opponent answers out loud.
  5. Click any card their answer rules out to knock it down. Your knocked-down cards are private.
  6. Pass the turn, and keep narrowing until you are sure. Then call their word — first correct call wins.

What makes it different

The deduction is the classic one, but the cards hold words rather than faces, so the questions open right up. A rack can mix animals, food, tools, places and weather, which means every question you ask has to earn its keep: is it alive splits the rack in half, is it red probably wastes your turn.

The game deliberately stays out of your conversation. It tracks the racks, whose turn it is and who called it — the questions and the bluffing are yours. Play it across a table, on a call, or on voice chat.

Room rules you can change

Card language
English or Arabic. Pick العربية and the whole rack is drawn from an Arabic word pool instead — everyday nouns, rendered in proper joined script, right to left. Both players get the same rack, so you only choose once, when you create the room.
Opponent's progress
Off by default, so you cannot see how many cards your opponent has knocked down. Turn it on for a more tactical race where you can see them closing in.
Sudden death
Off by default: a wrong call knocks that card down for you and passes the turn, so you can guess as often as you like. Turn it on and one wrong call loses the match outright.

Questions people ask

Is GuessRack free?

Yes — free, with no accounts, no downloads and no ads.

Do I need to install anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser on desktop, tablet or phone.

Can I play on a phone?

Yes. The rack switches to a taller layout on narrow screens.

Can I play GuessRack in Arabic?

Yes — set the card language to العربية when you create the room and the rack is drawn from an Arabic word pool, rendered in proper joined script.

What if my connection drops?

Refresh and you land back in the same match — your seat is held for five minutes, with your board exactly as you left it.

How long does a match take?

Usually five to ten minutes, and there is a rematch button at the end.
